Privacy Policy — JEE Ascend

Last updated: 2026

JEE Ascend is a personal JEE exam-preparation study tool. This page explains what information is collected when you use it, and how it's used.

What information is collected

When you first open the app, you're asked to register with:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your class (11, 12, or Dropper/Repeater)
  • The exam you're preparing for (JEE Main, or JEE Main + Advanced)

As you use the app, your study data is also stored — chapter progress, revision schedules, mock test scores, mistakes logged, streaks, habit check-ins, and reward points. This data stays linked to the email address you registered with.

How your information is used and stored

Your registration details and study data are stored in two places:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — used so your study data can be restored if you use the app on a different device, by entering the same email you registered with.
  • A Google Sheet — the app's developer uses this to keep a record of who has registered.

Your data is used only to run the app and to let its developer know who is using it. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond operating this app.

Data storage on your device

A copy of your data is also stored locally in your browser (or, if used inside the Claude app, in that app's storage) so the app works even without an internet connection.

No advertising, no analytics tracking

This app does not display ads, does not use advertising trackers, and does not use third-party analytics services.

Who can access your data

Only the developer of this app has access to the Firebase database and the Google Sheet described above. Because there is no login/password system, anyone who knows your registered email could technically look up data tied to that email — this app does not currently have strong account security, and it is not intended for storing sensitive personal information.

Requesting deletion of your data

To request that your data be deleted, email divyanshugupta9935@gmail.com with the email address you registered with, and it will be removed from the Google Sheet and Firebase database.

Who this app is for

JEE Ascend is intended for students preparing for JEE Main/Advanced (typically ages 15 and above). It is not directed at young children.

Contact
